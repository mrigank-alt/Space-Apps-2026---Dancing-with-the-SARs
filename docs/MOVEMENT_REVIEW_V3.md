# Movement review v3

This version prioritizes sensitivity and avoids interpreting unavailable uncertainty as evidence of stability. It does not establish higher scientific accuracy. All numeric movement thresholds remain exploratory pending independent validation.

- Grey: missing quality, displacement, or enough usable dates to assess persistence.
- Yellow: usable measurements with at least 1 cm/year absolute LOS velocity, at least 0.5 cm observed change, a within-farm deviation of at least 0.5 cm/year and 1.5 robust scales, or uncertainty that cannot establish small movement. Monotonic persistence is no longer required for yellow. Magnitudes detect either LOS direction; this does not identify downward subsidence.
- Green: interpretable, independently calibrated uncertainty, absolute velocity plus twice effective sigma below 1 cm/year, and absolute interval displacement below 0.5 cm. Effective sigma includes baseline estimation spread. Green does not certify safe infrastructure or eliminate systematic reference/atmospheric error.
- Red: interpretable calibrated uncertainty, absolute LOS velocity at least max(2 cm/year, 3 effective sigma), observed change at least 1 cm, persistence at least 0.5, and at least four adjacent red source cells. An isolated candidate becomes yellow. These gates are screening criteria, not damage thresholds.

Farm and plot summaries use geodesic area of clipped cells, with unmeasured boundary area added to grey. The class with the largest area determines the summary; ties prefer the stronger review class. At least three usable source cells are required; half or more of the area grey makes the summary grey. Minority red cells still require local review. Cell counts are retained for auditing but do not determine the area summary. Majority area can conceal localized hazards, so it must be read alongside the map.

Recommendations and the frontend action checklist now use the same backend-generated instructions, including coverage percentages, matched-date external comparisons, repeat levelling/GNSS, infrastructure inspection, and groundwater-manager review where relevant. No fixed pumping amount, recharge prescription, or damage deadline is inferred from satellite colours.

Monthly temperature and rainfall are requested from Open-Meteo historical reanalysis at a representative point in the GeoJSON boundary. The provider's returned grid coordinates are recorded alongside the requested point. Requests use a four-second HTTP timeout, no retry, and a 24-hour local cache. Failures return an explicit status without suppressing movement results. Partial months and incomplete rainfall are identified. Coordinates are sent to the weather provider; no NASA credentials are sent. Existing httpx is used, so no additional SDK is needed.

Weather context is not a subsidence simulator. The response explicitly returns no expected subsidence rate, states that a validated local displacement baseline is required, and does not change colours. Weather summaries over the measured interval are not long-term climatological normals. Correlation with seasonal temperature/rainfall cannot prove that movement is normal, harmless, or recoverable. A future seasonal comparison must match reference frame, dates and viewing geometry and be trained and evaluated on independent local displacement and hydrologic measurements.
